# The Lucky Coin (24 seconds, 3 scenes)

## Scene 1 (0–8s)
- **Visual:** An old man sits on a park bench at sunset. A girl of about 10 sits beside him, sad and holding a school paper marked "F".
- **Old man:** "Bad day, little one?"
- **Girl:** "I failed my math test. Again."
- **Sound:** Birds, gentle wind.

## Scene 2 (8–16s)
- **Visual:** He pulls an old, shiny gold coin from his coat and places it in her palm. She looks at it in wonder.
- **Old man:** "This coin is lucky. It helped me pass every test I ever took."
- **Sound:** Soft magical chime.

## Scene 3 (16–24s)
- **Visual:** A week later in a classroom, the girl holds up a test marked "A+" and grins. She flips the coin over and sees "1 Rupee, 2019" stamped on it. She smiles, realizing the truth.
- **Girl (whispering):** "It wasn't the coin… it was me."
- **Sound:** Uplifting music swell.
