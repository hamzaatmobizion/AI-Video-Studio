# The Stray Dog (40 seconds, 5 scenes)

## Scene 1 (0–8s)
- **Visual:** A rainy city street at evening. A small, wet, brown stray dog shivers under a shop awning, watching people hurry past.
- **Sound:** Heavy rain, distant traffic, a soft whimper.

## Scene 2 (8–16s)
- **Visual:** A teenage girl with a yellow umbrella stops, kneels down and holds out half of her sandwich. The dog hesitates, then slowly comes forward.
- **Girl (softly):** "Hey buddy… you hungry? It's okay."
- **Sound:** Rain on the umbrella.

## Scene 3 (16–24s)
- **Visual:** At her front door, the girl holds the dog wrapped in her jacket. Her father opens the door and frowns.
- **Father:** "Absolutely not. We talked about this."
- **Girl:** "Just one night, Papa. Please."
- **Sound:** Door creak.

## Scene 4 (24–32s)
- **Visual:** Later that night the father comes downstairs for water and finds the dog asleep on his slippers by the heater. He pauses, and his face softens.
- **Father (quietly, to himself):** "…Just one night."
- **Sound:** Ticking clock, a soft snore from the dog.

## Scene 5 (32–40s)
- **Visual:** The next morning, sunny. The father walks the dog on a new red leash, and the girl beams beside them. The dog's new tag reads "LUCKY."
- **Girl (teasing):** "Just one night, huh?"
- **Father (smiling):** "He's family now."
- **Sound:** Birds chirping, warm uplifting music.
