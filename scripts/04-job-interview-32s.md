# Job Interview (32 seconds, 4 scenes)

## Scene 1 (0–8s)
- **Visual:** A nervous young man in an oversized suit sits outside an office, bouncing his knee and reading his notes.
- **Young man (muttering):** "Stay calm. Strengths: hardworking. Weakness: …too hardworking."
- **Sound:** Clock ticking loudly.

## Scene 2 (8–16s)
- **Visual:** Inside, a stern female manager behind a big desk looks over his resume without smiling.
- **Manager:** "So. Why should we hire you?"
- **Young man:** "Because… I really, really need this job."
- **Sound:** Awkward silence, one cough.

## Scene 3 (16–24s)
- **Visual:** She raises one eyebrow and sets the resume down. He panics and accidentally knocks over her glass of water, which spills across the desk.
- **Young man:** "Oh no! I'm so sorry!"
- **Sound:** Glass tipping, water splash.

## Scene 4 (24–32s)
- **Visual:** He quickly pulls tissues from his pocket and cleans the whole mess carefully. She watches, then finally smiles.
- **Manager:** "You clean up your own mistakes. You start Monday."
- **Sound:** Happy, light music.
