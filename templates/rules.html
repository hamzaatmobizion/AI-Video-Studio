How to write a prompt for the best video

Videos are made in short clips that are joined together: 10-second clips in the Studio (Gemini), 8-second clips on the Flow screen (Google Flow). The app keeps them consistent; a few things only you can do.

The rules every clip gets

The app adds these to the end of every Flow clip prompt, so you don't need to write them. Copy them if you make a clip in Flow yourself or use another AI video tool.

The app also does this for you

  • Splits your story into clips, keeping every line you wrote. On Flow, a prompt written in time steps (0–8 seconds, 8–16 seconds…) keeps its actions, camera, lines and voices exactly as you wrote them; only what's missing, like where each object starts, is added. If a line goes missing or a clip has more than 20 words, the script waits for you to check it.
  • Keeps people, clothes, places and objects the same from clip to clip: portraits, a story bible, and each clip starting from the previous one's last frame.
  • Trims a cut to another shot from the end of a Flow clip.
  • Reads the length, style and 9:16 or 16:9 shape from your prompt, and leaves music out of the clips (add a Background music track instead).

What only you can do

RuleExample
Name one style, and no “realistic” in a cartoon. “Pixar-style 3D cartoon animation”
Use portraits in the video's style and clothes. A picked character looks exactly as in their portrait, clothes included, so don't describe their look in the prompt. Name them as they're named in your library. A Pixar-style portrait in the kurta they wear in the story
Keep lines short: about 14 spoken words per 8-second Flow clip with two people talking (20 at most), 25 per 10-second Gemini clip. Split a long speech across two clips.
One or two moments per clip. Say who does what, and which hand holds what. “holds the stick in her right hand and taps it to the beat”
Adults must look adult, and no photos of real people. “a man in his 30s with a thick mustache”
Write lines in the script of the language you want spoken. Pakistani Punjabi in Shahmukhi. “نی نوہے!” not “ਨੀ ਨੂੰਹੇ!”

Before you generate

  • Read the notes under the prompt: red will likely fail, yellow may hurt the video, green is fine.
  • Tick Check each clip: it catches extra people, changed clothes and wrong speakers, and remakes the clip once.

Ask an AI to write the prompt
Turn my idea (at the end) into one video prompt in exactly the format below. The video is made in 8-second clips.

- One or two moments per 8 seconds; at most about 14 spoken words per 8 seconds. Put each line in double quotes after who says it. Pakistani Punjabi in Shahmukhi script.
- Give each character an age, look, clothes with colours and a voice. Adults look clearly adult.
- Name one style. Say who does what and which hand holds what.
- Keep it plain: no lists of rules.

Format:

A [length]-second [vertical 9:16 or widescreen 16:9] [kind of video], [style].
Setting: [place, time of day, 2 or 3 key objects].

[Name]: [age], [hair, face, build], [clothes with colours].
Voice: [how they sound].
(one block for each character)

0–8 seconds: [one or two actions]. [Name] says: "[line]"
8–16 seconds: [one or two actions]. [Name] says: "[line]"
(continue in 8-second steps to the end)

Sound: [sound effects].

My idea: [write your idea here]

Example

A 16-second vertical 9:16 Pakistani Punjabi family comedy, Pixar-style 3D cartoon animation.
Setting: a colourful village kitchen in the afternoon, steel pots on a shelf, a wooden stool.

Saas: a woman in her 50s, grey hair in a bun, round face, orange shalwar kameez and green dupatta.
Voice: older woman, loud and expressive, Pakistani Punjabi accent.
Bahu: a woman in her late 20s, long black braid, pink shalwar kameez.
Voice: young woman, cheeky and sweet, Pakistani Punjabi accent.

0–8 seconds: Saas walks in, sees Bahu on her phone and puts her hands on her hips. Saas says: "نی نوہے! سارا دن موبائل تے لگی رہندی ایں!"
8–16 seconds: Bahu hides the phone behind her back and smiles. Bahu says: "امی جی، تہاڈے لئی رشتہ لبھ رہی آں!" Saas freezes, eyes wide.

Sound: a cartoon boing at the end.
