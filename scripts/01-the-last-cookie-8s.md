# The Last Cookie (8 seconds, 1 scene)

## Scene 1 (0–8s)
- **Visual:** A cozy kitchen at night. A small boy in pajamas tiptoes to the counter and reaches for the last cookie in a glass jar. The light snaps on. His mother stands in the doorway with her arms crossed, smiling.
- **Mother:** "Looking for something?"
- **Boy (frozen, cookie in hand):** "…It was calling my name."
- **Sound:** Light switch click, soft laugh.
