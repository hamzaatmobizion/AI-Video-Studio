# Lost Phone (16 seconds, 2 scenes)

## Scene 1 (0–8s)
- **Visual:** A young woman in a busy coffee shop frantically pats her pockets and checks under the table, looking panicked.
- **Woman:** "No, no, no… where is it? Everything's on that phone!"
- **Sound:** Coffee shop chatter, cups clinking.

## Scene 2 (8–16s)
- **Visual:** The barista leans over the counter and points calmly at her hand. She is holding the phone. She looks at it, then covers her face, embarrassed.
- **Barista:** "Ma'am… it's in your hand."
- **Woman:** "I'm going home."
- **Sound:** Barista chuckles.
